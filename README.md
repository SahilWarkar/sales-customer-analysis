# Sales & Customer Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

A Python-based business analysis project focused on understanding sales performance, customer behavior, regional performance, product categories, and year-over-year revenue trends.

The analysis was performed on **60,000 sales records** using Python to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The objective was to answer key business questions:

- How is revenue distributed across regions?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- How are online and offline channels performing?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which product categories are growing or declining?
- What areas represent potential business opportunities?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning
- Checked and handled data quality issues
- Converted date fields into usable datetime format
- Examined missing and duplicate records
- Created analytical variables for deeper analysis

### 2. Regional Analysis

Analyzed revenue, orders, and average order value across:

- North America
- Europe
- Asia Pacific
- Middle East & Africa
- Latin America

### 3. Sales Channel Analysis

Compared:

- Online
- Offline

Across orders, units, revenue, average order value, and discount levels.

### 4. Product Category Analysis

Analyzed performance across:

- Smartphone
- TV
- Home Appliance
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Wearable
- Audio

### 5. Year-over-Year Analysis

Compared sales performance across years and excluded incomplete 2026 data from the year-over-year comparison.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### Overall

- Dataset size: **60,000 records**
- Returns: **6.1%**
- 2025 revenue increased by approximately **3.7% compared with 2022**

### Regional Performance

- **North America:** $22.68M revenue
- **Asia Pacific:** Highest average revenue per order at approximately **$1,359**
- **Latin America:** $8.04M revenue

### Product Performance

**Smartphones** were the largest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately **41.55% of total revenue**.

### Product Growth

From 2022 to 2025:

- Laptop: **+11.67%**
- Audio: **+6.21%**
- Smartphone: **+5.55%**
- Home Appliance: **+2.59%**
- Wearable: **+2.72%**
- Tablet: **-1.14%**
- TV: approximately **flat**

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis indicates that the business has a strong smartphone revenue base and relatively stable performance across major regions.

However, some categories show stronger growth potential than others, while Tablet and TV performance requires closer monitoring.

The **Laptop category showed the strongest growth**, suggesting an opportunity to investigate whether additional investment or promotional activity could support further expansion.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

1. **Protect the smartphone revenue base** while identifying opportunities to increase customer value.

2. **Investigate the growth of laptops and audio products** and determine whether successful strategies can be expanded.

3. **Monitor declining or stagnant categories**, particularly Tablet and TV, and evaluate pricing, promotions, and customer demand.

4. **Continue monitoring regional performance** to identify markets where additional investment may generate growth.
